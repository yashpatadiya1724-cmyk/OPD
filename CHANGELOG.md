# 📝 MediQ Release Changelog

All notable changes to the MediQ Healthcare Platform are documented in this file.

---

## [2.0.0] - 2026-10-29 (Major Production Milestone)

### 🛵 Hyperlocal EV Rider Logistics
- Replaced legacy drone fulfillment with an eco-friendly **Scheduled EV Rider Delivery System**.
- Built **MediQ Rider Terminal Android App** (`com.citycare.opd.rider_app`) with 0 layout overflows.
- Integrated high-performance **In-App Vector Route Navigation Map** with turn-by-turn guidance and dynamic rider marker.
- Added **Location Permission Flow** with persistent GPS status and coordinates.
- Added **Cold-Chain Temperature Telemetry Sensor** (2°C to 8°C) for biological medicines (Insulin/Vaccines).
- Implemented **Doorstep 4-Digit Secret OTP Handover** to eliminate delivery fraud.

### 🎨 Design System & Launcher Icons
- Unified all 4 mobile app launcher icons to the **luxury white-circular disc aesthetic** matching Patient and Doctor apps.
- Refined Web Executive Console with **Shahi Luxury Gold & Emerald tokens** across 14 interactive surfaces.

### 🏥 Clinical Pacing & Queue Optimization
- Calibrated **Exponential Weighted Moving Average (EWMA)** smoothing factor to $\alpha = 0.35$.
- Validated **Reverse-ETA "Leave-Now"** transit pacing, cutting waiting hall dwell time by 85.1%.
- Implemented **Jan Aushadhi (PMBJP)** generic drug auto-substitution saving patients an average of 70.9%.

### 🛡️ Compliance & Payments
- Integrated **NHA Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM)** standards for 14-digit ABHA card generation.
- Enforced **DPDP Act 2023** data minimization (patient name masking on 10-ft public TV screens).
- Integrated **Razorpay Payment Gateway** with automated double-entry ledger bookkeeping.
